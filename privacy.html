Privacy Policy

OWLREEL studio. Last updated: 12 October 2026.

OWLREEL makes the animated short series LAST DELIVERY. We run a private publishing tool on the studio's own computer. It is used only by the studio, only with the studio's own Google account and YouTube channel (@owlreel). It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What access we ask Google for

The tool asks for three YouTube permissions (OAuth scopes), and uses each one only for the purpose below:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload the studio's own videos to the studio's own channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube: to manage the studio's own channel, for example to add an uploaded video to the studio's own playlist.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly: to read the view and engagement numbers (views, average view percentage, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained) of the studio's own videos, so we can see how our episodes perform.

What we do with the data

Data obtained through these permissions is used only to publish and measure the studio's own videos. We do not collect or read data about other people's accounts, viewers or videos.

  • Stored locally: the statistics are saved as files on the studio's computer. The Google sign-in token (refresh token) is kept in an encrypted local store on the same computer.
  • Not sold, not shared: we do not sell, rent, transfer or disclose this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • No humans read it except the studio owner, and only to review how our own videos are doing.

Google API Services User Data Policy

OWLREEL's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

You can remove the tool's access at any time on your Google account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops all access immediately. To have locally stored statistics or tokens deleted, email us at the address below.
